CONCLUSION

This project provided a practical understanding of the accounting procedures followed by a partnership firm. It demonstrated how business transactions are recorded in the Journal and transferred to Ledger Accounts before preparing the Trial Balance and final accounts.

Through the study of Apex Traders, the accounting treatment of partners' capital, drawings, interest on capital, interest on drawings and distribution of profits was understood. The preparation of the Trading Account, Profit and Loss Account, Profit and Loss Appropriation Account and Balance Sheet helped in understanding the overall financial position and performance of the firm.

Overall, this project highlighted the importance of maintaining accurate accounting records and following a systematic accounting process. It also helped connect theoretical concepts with their practical application in partnership accounting.
